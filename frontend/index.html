About AUREVIA

A studio built around fewer, better addresses

AUREVIA began in 2008 as a two-person practice matching architects with discerning buyers. Today we represent a small, carefully vetted portfolio of residences across the country — never more than we can know in detail.

Our mission is simple: treat every transaction the way we'd want our own home handled — with patience, discretion and a genuine eye for the building itself.
